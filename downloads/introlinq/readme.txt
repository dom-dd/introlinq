Connect your readers with bookable experts, automatically, and earn a share of the commission when they book.

== Description ==

IntroLinq scans your published articles and highlights relevant phrases, linking readers to bookable, vetted industry experts (entrepreneurs, investors, and more) hosted on IntroLinq's partner sites.

When a reader books an expert within 90 days of clicking, you receive 50% of the commission. There's no cost to install and no code editing required beyond entering your Publisher ID.

This plugin loads a single script from `https://www.introlinq.com/widget.js`, which performs the on-page matching and highlighting. No content or visitor data is sent to IntroLinq by this plugin itself; the widget script runs client-side in the visitor's browser. See [IntroLinq's privacy policy](https://www.introlinq.com/privacy) for details on the widget's own data handling.

You'll need a free IntroLinq publisher account at [introlinq.com](https://www.introlinq.com) to get your Publisher ID.

== Installation ==

1. Install and activate the plugin.
2. Go to Settings → IntroLinq in your WordPress admin sidebar.
3. Enter your Publisher ID (from your IntroLinq dashboard) and click "Save Publisher ID".
4. Visit any article with a few paragraphs of text - after a few seconds you should see phrases highlighted where a matching expert is available.

== Frequently Asked Questions ==

= Do I need to edit any code? =

No. After activating the plugin, entering your Publisher ID in Settings → IntroLinq is the only step required.

= Where do I get a Publisher ID? =

Sign up for a free account at [introlinq.com](https://www.introlinq.com) to get your Publisher ID.

= Does this slow down my site? =

The widget script loads asynchronously and only runs its matching logic on article pages with substantial content (150+ words).
