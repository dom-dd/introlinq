IntroLinq is partnered with OpenIntro to help bring more experts onto the platform - the people you suggest could end up matched on your own articles. If you know someone who'd be a great fit, suggest them below - or suggest yourself.

How this works

OpenIntro connects readers with experts for 1:1 bookings, and is expanding beyond business into a wide range of categories. Once you submit a suggestion, OpenIntro's team reviews it - if they think it's a fit, they'll contact that person directly to invite them to join (no form for the person to fill in). Either way, you'll hear back on the outcome.
