The Best Budget Wireless Earbuds You Can Buy Right Now

Wireless earbuds have gotten so good, and so cheap, that spending more than $100 barely buys you anything extra most people will notice in daily use. We tested a dozen pairs under $60 over the last month to find the ones actually worth your money.

What actually matters at this price

Battery life and fit matter more than marginal sound quality differences most people will never notice. A pair that falls out of your ears on a run is worse than a slightly duller-sounding pair that stays put for six hours straight.

Active noise cancellation used to be a $200+ feature exclusively. That's no longer true - several budget pairs now include a passable ANC mode, even if it's not going to match flagship headphones from Sony or Bose.

Our top pick and why

The clear standout combined a genuinely comfortable fit, eight-plus hours of battery on a single charge, and a companion app that actually works without needing an account. None of that is exciting, but it's exactly what most people actually want from a $45 pair of earbuds.

Call quality was the one place nearly every budget pair still struggled - wind noise in particular. If you take a lot of calls outdoors, it's worth reading reviews specifically about microphone performance rather than trusting the spec sheet.

What to skip

A few pairs we tested had genuinely excellent sound but paired unreliably, dropping connection mid-track more than once an hour. No amount of audio quality makes up for a pair of earbuds you can't trust to just work.
