How it works

01
Paste one line on a page

An "experts" or "get advice" page is the natural home. It takes the full width.

02
It builds the directory for your audience

Only the experts relevant to your topics, with search and filters.

03
A reader books, you earn 50%

The reader stays on your page to book. Paid monthly via Wise or PayPal.

Best for

  • A dedicated "work with an expert" or "our experts" page.
  • Resource hubs and pillar pages where readers arrive ready to act.
  • Publishers whose audience spans several topics - the filters do the sorting.
  • Sending newsletter or social traffic to a single, high-intent landing page.

The install

One line, on the page that should host the board. Replace YOUR_ID with the ID from your dashboard.

<script src="https://cdn.introlinq.com/expertboard.js" data-site="YOUR_ID"></script>
