How it works

A sidebar, an end-of-post slot, a homepage section. It sizes to its container.

Tracked to the post on that card. Paid monthly via Wise or PayPal.

Best for

  • A sidebar or a recurring slot between posts, where an inline widget can't reach.
  • Homepages and category pages that need a single, self-updating block.
  • Publishers who want the expert match visible without editing article bodies.
  • Running it alongside the in-article widget - the two don't compete.

The install

One line, placed where the block should appear. Replace YOUR_ID with the ID from your dashboard; set the heading there too.

<script src="https://cdn.introlinq.com/carousel.js" data-site="YOUR_ID"></script>
