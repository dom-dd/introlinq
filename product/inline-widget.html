How it works

01
Paste one line into your <head>

Once. From then on it runs on every article on the site - the archive included.

02
It picks the phrase and the expert

Per article, automatically. You never tag posts or choose who appears.

03
A reader books, you earn 50%

Tracked to the article that sent them. Paid monthly via Wise or PayPal.

Best for

  • Any blog - it is the default format, with no layout or design work.
  • Sites with a large back catalogue, where every old post starts earning at once.
  • Content where readers regularly hit a point of "I actually need to talk to someone".
  • Publishers who want monetization that stays inside the reading experience, not beside it.

The install

One line, before the closing </head> tag. Replace YOUR_ID with the ID from your dashboard.

<script src="https://cdn.introlinq.com/widget.js" data-site="YOUR_ID"></script>
