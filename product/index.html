IntroLinq reads your content, matches a vetted expert, and pays you 50% of the fee when a reader books. Pick the format that fits your site - or run several. They all share the same network and the same dashboard.

The four formats

Each takes the same input - your article, or a topic you paste in - and returns the best-matched expert. What changes is where it appears and how the reader meets it.

In-article widget

An underlined phrase inside your post. The reader hovers or taps and the matched expert appears - no layout work, runs on every article you have written.
